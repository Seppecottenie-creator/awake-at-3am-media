## Script

2. A Roman day always has twelve hours. In winter, an hour is forty-five minutes.
3. Public toilet? Long stone bench, no walls, zero privacy.
4. And a sponge on a stick. What it was for? Historians still can't agree.

6. Lunch is fish guts, left to rot in the sun. No tomatoes, so no pizza.
