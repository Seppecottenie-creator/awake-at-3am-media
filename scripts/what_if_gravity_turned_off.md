## Script

2. Nope. Nothing pushes you up. You'd just stand there.
3. Except Earth keeps spinning. At the equator, you drift up forty centimeters.
4. Then you drop. Like falling off a chair.

## Simplifications

- Thought experiment with rigid ground: in reality Earth's compressed rock and the air would also start to rebound once their weight disappeared. The script ignores this; only 5 seconds are covered.
- The hook shows what people imagine (everything flying off) and is immediately corrected by "Nope".
- The jump assumes you left the ground right when gravity switched off, and the landing ignores air resistance.
